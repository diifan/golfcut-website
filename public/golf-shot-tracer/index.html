What is a golf shot tracer?

A tracer draws the ball’s flight into your golf video, helping viewers follow the shot beyond the moment of contact. It makes the flight part of the viewing experience.

Add a tracer with GolfCut

GolfCut supports automatic ball-flight detection and adding a shot tracer to your video. Bring a memorable shot to life as part of a single-swing edit or a golf story.
