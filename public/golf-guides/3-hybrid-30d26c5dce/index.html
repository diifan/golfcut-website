3-iron versus 3-hybrid: make a comparison from your own shots

Compare a 3-iron and a 3-hybrid by the job you need the club to do. Record the lie, intended target and outcome of several shots with each club. A club number alone is not enough to establish which option will work better for your game.

Keep the comparison fair

Use comparable lies and targets. Note poor contact instead of deleting every miss: the spread of outcomes is part of the decision. If you have measured distances, keep carry and total distance separate.

Record the decision, not just the longest shot

Write down the shot you need most often, the trouble you want to avoid and which club gives you a usable result more consistently. Ask a qualified fitter or coach to interpret technical measurements; this guide does not prescribe a universal club choice.

Decision checklist

  • Name the shot you need from this slot in the bag.
  • Test both clubs from comparable lies.
  • Record misses alongside successful shots.
  • Compare measured outcomes without treating video effects as distance data.
