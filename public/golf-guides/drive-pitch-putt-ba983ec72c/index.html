Short-game recording

  1. Frame both the starting ball and the near landing area when possible, without placing the camera in the playing area.
  2. Keep the camera steady through contact. Sand, grass and the club can temporarily hide the ball.
  3. Separate visible flight from bounce and roll when reviewing an overlay. Do not extend a trajectory through an obstruction.

Plan what your own clip must show

Before recording, decide which part of drive pitch putt you want to review. Keep the observable event in one camera view. If the ball disappears or the clip changes angle, note that limit instead of filling the gap with an assumed flight.
