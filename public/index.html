A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you tee off.

What is GolfCut?

GolfCut is an AI golf video editor for turning golf footage into stories. It combines AI story editing, automatic Swing Cut, a ball flight tracer, and creative filters, effects, and transitions.

What AI stories can I create?

Choose from four story types: Best Swing, Highlight Reel, All Eyes on You, and Golf with Friends. Each gives your golf footage a different focus.

What does automatic Swing Cut do?

Swing Cut finds and clips individual golf swings from long recordings, helping you keep the action and leave out the waiting between shots.

Can I add a tracer to my golf video?

Yes. GolfCut supports adding a tracer to make the ball flight easier to follow in your golf videos.

Can I edit videos on this website?

This website introduces GolfCut’s app features. The interactive story and color examples are previews; video editing happens in the GolfCut app.
