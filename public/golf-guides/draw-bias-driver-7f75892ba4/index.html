Reviewing visible ball flight

  1. Record from a steady position behind the ball with room above the target line.
  2. Distinguish the launch direction from any later curvature you can actually see in the same camera view.
  3. A phone-video overlay does not diagnose the swing or measure a shot. Use the source guidance for technique context and keep the original footage for review.

Plan what your own clip must show

Before recording, decide which part of draw bias driver you want to review. Keep the observable event in one camera view. If the ball disappears or the clip changes angle, note that limit instead of filling the gap with an assumed flight.
