Plan your approach shot recording

Framing

Keep the ball and the space above the target in frame. A tight crop of the golfer can lose the flight immediately after impact.

Before pressing record

Record from a stable position behind the ball, clear of the swing. Keep recording through the finish; do not pan from the clubhead to the green.

What to check in the reference

Compare the visible launch with the visible flight, then mark where the ball becomes too small to follow. A broadcast cut to the green is a separate camera view; do not join those views into a continuous tracer.

Make a shot-by-shot viewing note

While watching Rory McIlroy in the linked reference, record where impact occurs, when the ball stops being visible and whether the camera cuts. The worksheet runs in this browser; its entries are not uploaded or used to infer a trajectory.

Try a tracer on your own clip

  1. Record a single shot using the framing checks above. Keep an unedited original.
  2. Trim the clip to 30 seconds or less for the online trial.
  3. Open GolfCut’s trial and mark the impact moment and starting ball position.
  4. Review the returned result. Detection may fail or contain errors; do not use an overlay as a distance measurement or proof of landing location.
